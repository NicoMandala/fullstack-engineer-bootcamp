# Assessment rubric (100 points)

- Correct API behavior and domain rules: 30
- Clean onion architecture boundaries: 15
- Meaningful automated tests and failure cases: 15
- Docker and CI/CD deployment: 15
- Logging, monitoring, diagnosis: 10
- AI-generated frontend integration and UX: 10
- Documentation, deadlines and independent explanation: 5

Required milestones: Day 3 python/API exercise; Day 5 two APIs; Day 10 3 passing CI pipeline runs and deployment; Day 15 trace three failures; Day 20 20 meaningful tests, deployed integrated app and demo. Passing smoke tests alone never means feature completion.
